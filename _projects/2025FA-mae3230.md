As part of my introductory fluids class, my lab group dissected a vacuum and applied our knowledge to estimate how fluid mechanics works in the vacuum. We created a YouTube video, which you can find here!

(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dp42T6aML0Q "Vacuums: Do They Suck?")

For my contribution, I assisted in the dissection of the project as well as collecting measurements and images of our process. I also researched explanations for our assumptions as well as explanations for fluid flow through pipes, elbows, orfices, and expansions/contractions. I assisted in writing and editing the script, and edited our final video and uploaded it to YouTube.
